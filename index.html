How to play

What is it about?

You design a bacterium and put it on an agar plate with opponents. After that everything runs on its own – you can only watch (and fast-forward). Whoever is left at the end wins.

Career

Every world gets its own bacterium. It starts with 12 upgrade points and the simplest movements. Every level has a special opponent – a real germ with a typical ability. Beat it and your bacterium gets 1 new upgrade point, and the ability is unlocked for good – also for the bacteria you create in later worlds. You can redistribute your points at any time.

Traits

Every trait has 10 steps and every step costs one point. Special abilities cost more the stronger they are. You will never have enough points for everything – choosing is the game.

Worlds

The career leads through real habitats, each with its own germs and its own look. You start on the skin: from harmless skin germs to an infected wound. Mouth & gut, water, soil and finally the hospital and bloodstream follow.

Stars, atlas and achievements

Every level has three stars: for winning, for a fast win (under 12 h) and for covering 25 % of the plate at some point. All three give a bonus point. Every germ you beat goes into your atlas with a picture and a fact, and special feats earn achievements.

Free play

Here you set the point budget yourself and can spend all points right away. Special abilities and special movements are only available once you have unlocked them in a career.

What happens on the plate?

Every coloured pixel is a bacterial cell. Cells eat the medium below them (bright = lots of food, dark = eaten up), move according to their pattern, divide when they have enough energy, and die of hunger or old age. When a cell runs into an enemy, they fight: attack against defense plus a little luck.

Spotting special abilities

Capsule: cells look brighter while their slime layer is intact. Spores are pale, almost white dots. Biofilm is a dark, patterned mat. Toxins show as a cloud around the colony.

End of the game

A round lasts at most 48 hours of incubation. You win if you are the last culture alive, make up almost all cells for a long time – or have the most cells when time runs out.

Install on your iPhone

Open in Safari, tap Share and choose “Add to Home Screen”. Agava then starts like a full-screen app and also works offline. Your bacteria are only saved on this device.
