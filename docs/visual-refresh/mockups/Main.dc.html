01 · COLOUR Cool ink, not muddy olive Navy-black ground, neutral greys. Saturated mint, violet and gold accents that read as light.
02 · MATERIAL Glass surfaces with depth Translucent panels, hairline light borders, top highlights and soft shadows instead of flat grey boxes.
03 · TYPE A display face with character Unbounded for titles and scores, Manrope for UI, JetBrains Mono for stats and timers.
04 · THE PLATE Lit, glossy, sharp Native-resolution canvas, organic colony edges, specular glass, light that glows from the agar.
05 · MENUS Game-first hierarchy Big hero plate, one glowing CTA, world cards with art, a floating tab bar. Fewer list rows.
06 · MOTION Juice on every reward Spring presses, star bursts, number roll-ups, glow pulses — all collapsing under reduced motion.
