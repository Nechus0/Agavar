# Agava – the first 100 levels: 5 worlds × 20 levels

Each world is **one real habitat** with its own look (plate colours), its own
**plate mechanic** and only germs that really live there. Every level's special
opponent is a real germ; beating it unlocks its typical trait for your bacterium.

The journey: the outside of the body (skin) → inside the body (mouth & gut) →
nature (water, soil) → the hospital and finally the bloodstream, where the immune
system fights back. More habitats follow as expansions after level 100.

| World | Levels | Look | Plate mechanic | New abilities |
|-------|--------|------|----------------|---------------|
| 1 Skin 🖐 | 1–20 | peach / skin tone | pores (food spots), cuts (food stripe) | Sit, Shuttle, Tumble, Capsule, Spores, Snake/Eight, Biofilm, Toxins · then Sticky, Sebum eater |
| 2 Mouth & Gut 🌀 | 21–40 | enamel white → warm brown | sugar waves (mouth), acid (stomach), flow (gut) | Acid, Swarming, Plasmid |
| 3 Water 🌊 | 41–60 | green pond → blue sea | day/night light cycle, currents, salt | Photosynthesis, Magnetotaxis, Quorum sensing |
| 4 Soil 🌱 | 61–80 | dark brown | stones block squares, roots are food lines, compost heat | Predator, Antibiotics |
| 5 Hospital & Blood 🏥🩸 | 81–100 | clinical white → dark red | antibiotic zones, then blood flow + hunting white blood cells | Resistance, Persister cells, Stealth |

**Expansions after level 100:** Kitchen 🧀 (cold zones, fermentation),
Hot Springs 🌋 (heat zones, radiation), Space Station 🚀 (microgravity).

### Structure of every world (20 levels)
- **Chapter A (levels 1–10 of the world):** introduces the habitat and its mechanic,
  ends with a **chapter boss** (level 10).
- **Chapter B (levels 11–20):** harder variants, two to three opponents,
  ends with the **world boss** (level 20): a famous germ with two abilities.
- Levels 1–3 of each chapter have one opponent, then two, at the end three.
- Every world has its own plate colours, food layouts, a short intro and its own
  page in the atlas, so each habitat feels like a collection to complete.
- New abilities come mostly in chapter A; chapter B adds one or two.

## World 1 – Skin 🖐 (levels 1–10 playable)

*From a harmless touch to an infected wound.*

- **Look:** skin-toned plate (peach when full, dark when eaten up).
- **Mechanic:** food layouts *Pores* (many small rich spots: hair follicles and
  sweat glands) and *Cut* (a rich stripe of blood and plasma).

## World 2 – Mouth & Gut 🌀 (levels 21–40)

*Sugar on the teeth, darkness in the gut.*
- **Chapter A – Mouth & stomach:** plaque and cavities (*Streptococcus mutans*,
  *Actinomyces*, *Fusobacterium*, *Veillonella*, *Porphyromonas gingivalis* as chapter
  boss), stomach (*Helicobacter pylori*).
- **Chapter B – Gut:** *Bacteroides*, *Bifidobacterium*, *E. coli*, *Enterococcus*,
  *Salmonella*, *Vibrio cholerae*; world boss *Clostridioides difficile* (spores + toxins).
- **Mechanics:** sugar waves (food floods the plate, then dries up), acid, flow
  (food drifts in one direction).
- **Unlocks:** Acid, Swarming, Plasmid (copy a trait from killed cells).

## World 3 – Water 🌊 (levels 41–60)

*From the garden pond to the open sea.*
- **Chapter A – Pond & river:** *Synechococcus*, *Anabaena*, *Caulobacter*,
  *Magnetospirillum*, *Bdellovibrio*; chapter boss *Legionella*.
- **Chapter B – Ocean:** *Prochlorococcus*, *Pelagibacter*, *Aliivibrio fischeri*
  (glows), *Vibrio vulnificus*; world boss *Vibrio parahaemolyticus*.
- **Mechanics:** day and night (photosynthesis only by day), currents, salt.
- **Unlocks:** Photosynthesis, Magnetotaxis, Quorum sensing.

## World 4 – Soil 🌱 (levels 61–80)

*Crumbs, roots and chemical warfare.*
- **Chapter A – Garden soil & roots:** *Bacillus subtilis*, *Rhizobium*,
  *Pseudomonas putida*, *Myxococcus xanthus*; chapter boss *Bdellovibrio*.
- **Chapter B – Compost & caves:** heat-loving *Thermus*, *Clostridium*,
  *Nocardia*; world boss *Streptomyces* (the antibiotic factory).
- **Mechanics:** stones block squares, roots are lines of food, compost heat.
- **Unlocks:** Predator, Antibiotics.

## World 5 – Hospital & Blood 🏥🩸 (levels 81–100)

*Antibiotics, and then the immune system.* The finale.
- **Chapter A – Hospital:** MRSA, *Pseudomonas*, *Acinetobacter baumannii*,
  *Klebsiella pneumoniae*, VRE; chapter boss multi-resistant *Klebsiella*.
- **Chapter B – Bloodstream:** *Neisseria meningitidis*, *Streptococcus pneumoniae*,
  *Salmonella Typhi*, *Yersinia pestis*; final boss: sepsis-causing *S. aureus*.
- **Mechanics:** antibiotic zones kill non-resistant cells; then fast blood flow and
  white blood cells that hunt any cell they touch.
- **Unlocks:** Resistance, Persister cells, Stealth – and the final title.

### Monetisation fit
World 1 (20 levels) is free. Worlds 2–5 are the full version (one purchase).
Expansions after level 100 can be released as free updates, which keeps the game
visible in the App Store.

### Atlas
The atlas is sorted by world. Each germ shows a picture, where it lives, a fact and
what it unlocks. Photos from Wikimedia Commons can be added with
`node tools/fetch-germ-photos.mjs` (needs network access to Wikipedia); the tool
stores the author and licence of every image so they can be credited in the app.
